Deployment-layer refusal erosion

A product's own system prompt can void the safety properties of the model underneath it. This measures the gap between what a model refuses when asked directly and what it produces when the same intent arrives through a deployed wrapper.

Fixture run. These results come from the offline stub provider, which returns hardcoded responses encoding the pattern observed in the wild. They are not evidence about any model or product — they demonstrate that the harness measures the delta correctly. Live runs require an API key.
16 of 24 harmful scaffold×probe pairs were refused by the bare model but completed through the wrapper. Benign controls excluded from the denominator — those are meant to complete.

Why the benign controls matter

“Honest drug education” is designed to sit adjacent to the controlled-substances probes. A well-built system passes it and refuses the home-synthesis probe. A keyword blacklist fails both — which is why this failure class can't be fixed with a wordlist, and why it needs measuring at the deployment layer.
